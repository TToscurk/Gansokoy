# Human Village Art Direction

Read the current production state in `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` and the relevant guidance in `docs/ningen-no-sato.md` and `docs/village-art-direction.md`. Preserve approved layout and subsystem invariants unless the task explicitly changes them.

## Contract

- Express one shared architectural culture with visibly different households, businesses, and social functions.
- Use a Japanese early-modern to early-Meiji base without treating the village as a strict reconstruction museum.
- Let Gensokyo absorb restrained cross-era or outside-world details when they feel naturally adopted into village life.
- Avoid modern municipal design, theme-park cleanliness, excessive uniformity, and clone-town repetition.
- Make facade identity legible through architecture and functional props, not floating labels alone.
- Let buildings, vegetation, and ground edges respond to human use: access, work, drainage, wear, storage, privacy, and maintenance.
- Preserve facade rhythm, street hierarchy, composed setbacks, and sightlines to the fire lookout, shrine/torii, market, bridges, and other landmarks.
- Place each prop for a believable function. Prefer restrained clusters and useful junction details over random decoration.
- Keep the village readable as Touhou/Gensokyo lived-in fantasy while retaining coherent timber, plaster, kawara, and machiya proportions.

Pair this contract with `$godot-visual-review` for any implementation or visual acceptance pass, and with `$blender-asset-production` when mesh identity must change.
